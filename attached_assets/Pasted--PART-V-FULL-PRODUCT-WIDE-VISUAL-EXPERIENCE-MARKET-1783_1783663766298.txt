- components;

Reserved for:

- major section transitions;

- major X portals;

- selected spatial scenes;

For:

- AI thinking;

- agent execution;

- validation;

- build;

- deployment;

- Git changes;

- synchronization;

- connection;

- loading;

- success;

- warning;

- error.

Never fake activity.

Never animate a success state before success is verified.
